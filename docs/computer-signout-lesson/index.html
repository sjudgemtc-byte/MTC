Computer Sign-Out Form Lesson

Learn how to check out a classroom computer, find the device number, complete the Google Form correctly, and return the device responsibly.

Lesson steps

Privacy reminder: only enter the information requested by the form. Do not enter passwords, banking details, Medicare details, addresses, or private family information.
Step 1

Why do we sign out computers?

When you borrow a computer or laptop for class, MTC needs a simple record of who is using it and when it is returned. This helps keep equipment available, safe, and ready for the next person.

What the form records

  • Your name
  • Your class or program
  • The date
  • The computer or laptop number
  • The time signed out
  • The time returned

Good workplace habits

  • Follow the sign-out process
  • Use the correct device number
  • Return the device on time
  • Report damage or problems honestly
Discussion: What could happen in a workplace if equipment is borrowed but not signed out?
Step 2

Find the computer or laptop number

Before you open the form, look at the device and find its number. The form says the number is on the bottom of the computer.

  1. Carefully turn the laptop or device if needed.
  2. Look for a label, sticker, asset number, laptop number, or computer number.
  3. Write the number exactly as it appears.
  4. If you cannot find it, ask Simon before submitting the form.
Accuracy tip: check the number twice. One wrong digit can make the record hard to follow up.

Complete the real Google Form

Open the form and enter your details carefully. Use the student form link, not the editing link.

Before you submit: check your name, class, date, device number, sign-out time, and return time. If you are not sure what to enter for return time, ask Simon before submitting.
Step 5

Check understanding

Answer these quick questions after you complete the form.

1. Why do we complete a computer sign-out form?

2. What should you do if you cannot find the laptop number?

3. What information should you avoid entering into the form?

Trainer notes

Suggested delivery

Time: 20-30 minutes. Level: Beginner digital literacy / workplace preparation.

  • Model the form on the projector before students complete it.
  • Ask students to locate the device number before opening the form.
  • Support students who are unsure about time entry, spelling, or class name.
  • Evidence opportunity: student completes the online sign-out form with appropriate support.

Suggested Salesforce wording: The participant practised completing an online workplace-style equipment sign-out form. The participant identified required fields, located the device number, entered relevant information, and submitted the form with trainer support where required.
