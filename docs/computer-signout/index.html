Complete the computer sign-out form

Use this form when you take a computer or laptop for class. You may need to sign in with your email because the form collects email responses.

Open Computer Sign-Out Form

1. Find the number

Look for the computer or laptop number on the bottom of the device.

2. Complete the form

Enter your name, class, date, device number and sign-out time. If the form asks for return time before you return it, ask Simon what to enter.

3. Return the device

Return the computer in good condition and tell Simon if there is any issue.

Do not enter private information beyond what the form asks for. Do not change another student’s response. Do not use the form editor link.
