Using the ABC Website

Practise opening a trusted Australian website, using menus and search, reading online information, and recording useful facts.

All activities ABC Website Activity

Lesson steps

Approx. time: 90-120 minutes.
Skills: internet navigation, search, reading, note-taking, short written answers and partner discussion.
Part A

Open the ABC website

The ABC website has news, videos, radio, podcasts, local information, sport, lifestyle, quizzes and emergency information.

  1. Open Chrome or another browser.
  2. Click in the address bar at the top.
  3. Type www.abc.net.au
  4. Press Enter.
Open ABC Website
Class question: Where do you normally get your news or everyday information?
Part B

Find these ABC sections

Look around the ABC website. Tick each section when you find it.

Part C

Choose and read one article

Choose one article from News, Sport, Lifestyle, Science, Local News, Weather or Emergency information.

Part D

Use ABC search

Use the ABC search bar to search for one useful topic.

Search ideas: Sydney weather, Centrelink, jobs, health, scams, sport, food, rent or transport.
Extension

Reliable information check

Answer Yes or No for the article you chose.

Does the article show a date?
Does the article have a clear headline?
Is the article from a known Australian website?
Is the information useful to me?
Would I share this information with someone else?
Part E

Reflection

Answer in one or two sentences.

Trainer guide

Using the ABC Website for News, Information and Everyday Learning

Suggested timing: 90-120 minutes. Class: Adult Digital Literacy / SEE.

SMART learning objectives

  • Open the ABC website by typing www.abc.net.au into a browser.
  • Identify at least three main sections of the ABC website.
  • Use ABC search to find information on one topic.
  • Open one article and record the headline, topic, date and one key fact.
  • Explain whether the information is useful, reliable or relevant.

Evidence opportunities

  • Completed online or printed worksheet notes
  • Trainer observation checklist
  • Screenshot of article found
  • Short written summary
  • Oral explanation to partner
  • Search terms recorded by student

Whiteboard instructions

  1. Open Chrome.
  2. Type www.abc.net.au
  3. Find ABC News.
  4. Choose one article.
  5. Write the headline, topic, date, one fact and one new word.
  6. Use Search to find one topic.
  7. Tell your partner what you learned.

Suggested Salesforce wording: The participant practised navigating the ABC website, locating website sections, using search, reading an online article, and recording key information. The participant completed the task with trainer support as required.
