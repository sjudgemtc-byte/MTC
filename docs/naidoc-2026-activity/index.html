NAIDOC Week 2026: 50 Years of Deadly

Explore the official NAIDOC website, learn what the 2026 theme means, and practise respectful online research. This activity is designed for adult learners and can be completed individually or with support.

Activity steps

Key idea: Learn from First Nations-led and official sources. Listen carefully, use respectful language and do not copy artwork without permission.
60–90 minutes

Start here

National NAIDOC Week celebrates and recognises the history, cultures and achievements of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. In 2026, it was held from 5–12 July.

Learning goals

Navigate an official website, identify key information, take brief notes, compare themes and credit your sources.

You will need

A browser, this activity page, headphones if using audio, and a document or paper for extra notes.

Respectful-content notice

The official NAIDOC website advises Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people that it may contain images and voices of deceased people. You may skip any item, ask the trainer for an alternative, or work with a partner.

Website research

What is NAIDOC Week?

  1. Open the official National NAIDOC Week page.
  2. Find one sentence that explains the purpose of the week.
  3. Rewrite the idea in your own words. Do not copy a full paragraph.

Support option

Use the website's Listen feature, read one paragraph at a time, or ask the trainer to model how to find the main idea.

2026 theme

Explore “50 Years of Deadly”

The official theme page describes the milestone as a tribute to Elders, organisers, artists and communities, and as a time to look back at stories, marches, languages, art and leadership.

Look back at earlier themes

Challenge option

Explain how one earlier theme connects with “50 Years of Deadly”. Use two or three sentences.

Choose one activity

Create and share respectfully

Option A: Digital timeline

Create three timeline entries: an earlier NAIDOC theme, a middle year, and 2026. Add a short source credit.

Option B: Theme word cloud

Choose 8–12 respectful words from the official theme page. Highlight three and explain why they matter.

Option C: Community event search

Find a NAIDOC event from an official organisation. Record the organiser, location, date and source link.

Option D: 60-second explanation

Prepare a short spoken explanation of the 2026 theme using your own words and two accurate facts.
